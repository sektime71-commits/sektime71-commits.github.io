Check this before you spend a review request

Each failed review adds a cooldown, and accounts get a limited number of attempts. Google rarely says what triggered the suspension, so the safest move is to close every mismatch first. This list covers the common causes in Google's Misrepresentation policy and in real Shopify Community threads.

No checklist guarantees reinstatement. Google makes the final call, and some suspensions are permanent. Use this to avoid wasting an attempt on something fixable.

01One business, the same everywhere

Google cross-checks who you are. Small differences between places read as a trust problem.

02Policies a customer can actually use

Generic or missing policies are one of the most common findings.

03Feed = product page = checkout

Compare a sample of products field by field. One mismatch can be enough.

04Product identifiers

Easy to overlook, especially for own-brand products.

05Claims you can back up

Misrepresentation is literally about misleading shoppers.
