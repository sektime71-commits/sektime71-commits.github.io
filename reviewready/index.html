What gets checked

01Business identity

Name, address, email and phone match across Merchant Center, contact page, policies and checkout. Domain claimed, no redirects to myshopify.com.

02Policies

Shipping, returns and refunds, privacy and terms exist, are linked in the footer and are not template text from another store.

03Feed = page = checkout

Price, currency and availability in your product data match what shoppers see on each product page and in its structured data.

04Product identifiers

Real GTINs on branded products; own-brand items handled with identifier_exists and a brand value.

05Claims and pricing

Compare-at ("was") prices, sale timers, "#1" and "official" claims, reviews and delivery promises you may not be able to back up.

06Before you press "Request review"

What's fixed and live, what's only planned, and what to note down so a failed review still teaches you something.
